# Glossary
- Companion: a befriended creature with its own health, focus, experience and level.
- Party: up to three companions ready for battle.
- Sanctuary: all other companions, available at settlements.
- Focus: energy spent on elemental moves; restored by rest.
- Net: one consumable attempt to befriend a wild creature.
- Sigil: a warden's recognition, opening the next island.
- Warden: a guardian whose challenge tests an elemental lesson.
- Lantern: the final beacon restored by the expedition.
