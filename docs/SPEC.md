# Tidebound acceptance criteria
An original compact creature collecting RPG in the Lantern Isles, with no copied names, assets, maps, dialogue or music. Three starters, ten creatures, connected explorable habitats and settlements, a rival, three wardens, a finale and an ending. Party of three and a sanctuary for up to 1,000 companions (capacity checked before any net is spent). Journal, elemental guide, XP, levels and unlocked moves. Free healing and basic replenishment prevent softlocks; atomic shops, potions, capture items and battle actions.

Public seams authorized by delegation: newGame, act (the sole gameplay command boundary), encodeSave/decodeSave. Test seeded battle turns, energy costs, type effects, status, switch/forced switch, capture/storage, purchases, defeat, rewards, gates and all starter campaigns. Menus never submit turns. Fainted actors cannot act. Rewards granted at battle resolution once. Capture odds 25–90%, one net spent, wardens cannot be captured. Saves are versioned and validated: ongoing battle resumes exactly, seed included; corrupt or inconsistent saves rejected without crashing. No asynchronous combat callbacks.

Visible browser acceptance: complete campaign using UI, capture, switch, shop, heal, recovery, reload, repeated interactions, modal input isolation, mobile viewport. Report physical-device coverage separately. Audio opt-in synthesized original tones. No accounts or paid services. Private GitHub with CI; local session server preserves ports 5173–5180.
